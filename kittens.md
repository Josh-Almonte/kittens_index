## How to Add Your Kitten

1. Open this file in your text editor.
2. Copy the last row in the table and paste it as a new row at the bottom.
3. Fill in your kitten's details and your own name in the **Added By** column.
4. Save the file, then follow the steps in `README.md` to commit and push.

**Example row to copy:**
```
| 4 | Mittens    | Grey         | Toy mouse           | Your Name       |
```
